# Judgment

This page renders `lofi.judgment`: the judgment fit declaration
(`JudgmentFitSpec`) and its response, inference, calibration and comparison
blocks, judgment designs and their codecs, the response compiler and the NUTS,
variational and MAP fitters, prior predictive calibration
(`CalibrationCampaign`, `ReplicateRecord`), held-out splitting and prediction,
and the cumulative family and ordinal head.
[Fit a judgment model](../how-to/judgments/fit-a-judgment-model.md) shows them
in use.

[Judgment models](../explanation/judgment-models.md) defines the ordinal,
choice, and bounded response likelihoods, links, identification constraints,
marginalization, and posterior objectives used by these interfaces.
